Privacy Policy

Your music library stays on your device.

LYRA is built around local-first processing with no ad networks and no data brokers.

1. Local Audio & Playlists

LYRA uses Android's READ_MEDIA_AUDIO (or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 8–12) solely to scan and play audio files stored on your phone. Your local tracks, folders, and playlists are never uploaded to external servers.

2. On-Device AI & Translations

Music Copilot intent matching, Music Lab Equalizer settings, and ML Kit lyric translations run directly on your Android device. Search prompts inside Music Copilot do not require cloud AI keys.

3. LYRA Radio, Lyrics & Optional Sign-In

When online, LYRA connects to LYRA Radio servers to stream curated radio tracks and artwork, and queries LRCLIB for synced lyrics. If you sign in with Google, Microsoft, or a LYRA username, only basic identity/account state is used for your profile.

4. Website & Zero Ad Trackers

Neither the LYRA Android app nor this website runs third-party ad SDKs or invasive cross-site tracking cookies. Any optional website traffic metrics use cookieless privacy-friendly analytics only.
