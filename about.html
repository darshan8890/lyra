Why LYRA Exists

Calm obsidian design meets local intelligence.

LYRA started with a simple goal: an offline music player shouldn’t feel stuck in 2012. Every screen in LYRA — from dynamic artwork-tinted player backdrops and live waveform Home Screen widgets to shareable lyric cards — is crafted to feel calm, tactile, and deliberate.

When you want something new, LYRA Radio lets you stream curated tracks with artist portraits and save favorites offline for commutes or flights. When you’re offline, your entire library, playlists, Music Copilot, Music Lab Equalizer, and lyric translations keep working without touching the cloud.
