    Frequently asked questions.

    Is it safe to install LYRA directly from this website?

    Yes. This is the official direct APK package (com.aura.music) built directly from the LYRA Android project. You can verify the exact SHA-256 checksum listed in the Download section above, and LYRA contains zero ads or third-party trackers.

    Why does Android show a “Install unknown apps” prompt?

    Android displays that standard notice whenever you install an app outside Google Play. Allowing your browser once lets Android complete the installation — and you can turn the toggle back off right afterward if you like.

    Does LYRA work completely offline?

    Yes. Your local music files, playlists, Music Copilot, Music Lab Equalizer, and on-device lyric translation all work offline. When you are online, LYRA Radio lets you stream new tracks and save favorites for offline listening too.

    Which Android versions and audio formats are supported?

    LYRA runs on Android 8.0 (Oreo) through Android 15 and uses Media3 ExoPlayer to play MP3, M4A/AAC, FLAC, WAV, OGG, and Opus tracks smoothly with gapless playback and hardware audio routing.

    How do I update LYRA when a new version comes out?

    Simply download the newer APK from this page and install it over your existing LYRA app — your playlists, library, and account stay intact.
